Where you are

Adds your country's CERT, regulator and police channels. The CERT can lean on a host and registrar in ways an individual reporter cannot.

Your reporting accounts

Add every address you report from and switch between them on the Report tab. The chosen one is used for the vendor forms, the From: on the complaint drafts, and the Gmail compose link.

The Gmail link picks the account by address (authuser=), so it works without you knowing any slot number. Leave Gmail slot blank unless you have a reason to pin the /u/N/ path — a slot that does not exist in your browser makes Gmail fall back to /u/0/ and send from the wrong account.

API keys — optional, both free

Blank just means those two get skipped. Stored in config.json, chmod 600.

SMTP — optional, sends the complaint emails for real

Leave blank and you get .eml files instead; open them in Mail and hit send. Gmail needs an app password, not your account password.

Pages you have reported

One case file per target, kept in reports/<host>/case.json. Timestamped proof of what went where — this is what ICANN wants 24h after the registrar ignores you.
